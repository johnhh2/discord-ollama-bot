Most depended-on nodes

Why economy is the hub

Balances live in state.economy["users"] keyed by user id alone, with no guild dimension. Every gambling game, crime, shop purchase, bounty, chess reward, deed and AI-thread charge moves coins through add_balance / deduct_balance, and every result is reported back through record_gambling_event, whose GAMBLING_RESULT_HOOKS is how the !session thread learns its leader. Insurance, savings, the lottery pot, the 5am CT gameplay day and the records system all live in the same module.

Two-way ties inside the service layer

economy imports artifacts and properties for revenue and rate effects, and both import economy back (lazily, inside functions). leveling and level_unlocks do the same. These cycles are tolerated with function-local imports; a new service should follow that pattern rather than a top-level import.

The one feature that stays off the money

Idle RPG has its own per-guild gold and never touches coins: the wallet is global, so paying gold out as coins would multiply across servers. Its only economy import is the gameplay-day clock. Counters, voice pings, Minecraft and status are likewise coin-free server tools.

Gates every feature passes through

core runs a bot-wide permission gate and level gate on every command, and permissions.is_silenced is the single blocklist check that reaction and message handlers must call themselves. That is why 26 nodes import permissions: it is a cross-cutting concern, not a feature dependency.

Background loops

Eleven tasks.loop timers: the economy minute loop (insurance sweep), dailies refresh, lottery draw check, scratchoff cap reset, session renames, bounty expiry (2 min), leveling XP tick (5 min), graph snapshots (30 min), idle tick, Minecraft poll, status rotation.

Message side-effect chain

events.on_message is the other entry point: XP grant, ragebait, mock, tax, curse, auto-daily, then blackjack / puzzle / hangman input, then AI routing. Bots are excluded from the side effects; reactions bypass it entirely, which is why reaction handlers carry their own blocklist checks.
